# Counseling language rules

## Preserve certainty

| Evidence state | Suitable language |
|---|---|
| Reviewed pathogenic/likely pathogenic result with phenotype fit | “The result supports/establishes a molecular diagnosis of…” according to laboratory policy |
| VUS | “The significance is currently uncertain and this result does not establish or exclude the diagnosis.” |
| Negative | “No causative variant was identified within the scope of this test.” |
| Assay-blind hypothesis | “The phenotype raises the possibility of…; targeted testing would be required to confirm or exclude it.” |
| Unreviewed automation | “Automated interpretation proposal requiring qualified review.” |

Do not replace these distinctions with “positive” or “negative” alone.

## Inheritance and family implications

- State inheritance only for the relevant reviewed gene-disease association.
- Separate variant inheritance in this family from the disorder's general mode.
- Do not infer parental status from absence of parental results.
- Do not give a numeric recurrence risk without the necessary diagnosis,
  inheritance, penetrance, parental/phase, and mosaicism context.
- A VUS generally should not be used alone for predictive testing of unaffected
  relatives or irreversible clinical decisions.
- Explain what segregation testing can and cannot resolve.

## Patient-facing version

Use short sentences and define technical terms. Explain uncertainty directly.
Avoid implying blame, certainty about future symptoms, or that a negative result
rules out a genetic cause. Preserve all limitations present in the clinician
version.

## Review status

End every draft with the reviewer role required, unresolved questions, and a
statement that the text has not been signed or entered into the clinical record.
